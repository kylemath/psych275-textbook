How It Works

Paste a practice question and your answer below. This tool generates a grading prompt that you can paste into ChatGPT to receive feedback similar to what you'd get on an actual quiz. No data is sent anywhere — the prompt is generated entirely in your browser.

📖 Next Steps

  1. Click "Copy to Clipboard" to copy the generated prompt
  2. Go to chatgpt.com (you can use it without an account)
  3. For Quick mode: GPT-4o mini works great (free tier compatible)
  4. For Detailed mode: Use GPT-4o or GPT-4 for best results (longer prompt)
  5. Paste the prompt and press Enter
  6. Review the feedback to understand your strengths and areas for improvement
